About

We run a small UK online retail business and keep our own records of dealing with UK wholesalers. This site publishes those records as they are, with the date, the way each figure was counted and nothing borrowed from anyone else.

Each figure on a page is a count from our own records. Where a figure could mislead, the page says how it was counted.
