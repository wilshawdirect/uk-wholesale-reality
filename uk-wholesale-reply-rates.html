What share of UK wholesalers answer a cold trade-account email

Published by ADZ. Data as of 19 October 2026.

The numbers

MeasureValue
1.1% of the wholesalers with a first email on record replied at least once (n=725).1.1%
2.6% of the wholesalers with a first email on record had at least one email bounce (n=725).2.6%
Wholesalers with a first email on record that never answered (no reply, no bounce): 698.698

Where these numbers come from

Counts from our own records of dealing with UK wholesalers. We do not use data from other companies on this page.

How we measured it

Measured on wholesalers with a first cold email on record; a status-only row (applied via sign-up, then we wrote) is excluded; a bounce is the receiving server's rejection; an automatic reply, out-of-office or help-desk acknowledgement is not a reply (when the newest inbound is one, the wholesaler counts only if it has replied more than once).
